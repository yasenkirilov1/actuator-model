Every input result file and its native model/recipe are hash-bound in the JSON index. Later studies retain every failed full native trace in their private directory; early studies retain first/best/worst traces only. Source model versions remain distinct.

Initial external spring topology tuning comprises 126 runs on earlier inertial controller versions. It does not establish that all spring designs fail: the measured-motion feedforward and robot observer require separate qualification for compliant links. The subsequent lower-spring studies fairly tune larger PI gains, filtered PID and a proper causal B-inertia inverse before selecting a compact spring/damper candidate. The four-rate full spring grid has not been executed on the final controller.

The V6 pilots compare 40–110 MN/m structural cell rates with zero assumed structural damping, no external springs, 2 kg and 0.1 kg tools, 50 N, 90°, and a finite seed cohort. All 72 V6 runs retained contact and completed; force errors above 0.5 N remain failures. These stiffness values and hypothetical damping cases are design sensitivities, not manufacturer stiffness tolerances or damping specifications.

The selected lower-series spring/damper and proper B-inertia inverse feedforward need repeated-bump, numerical refinement, actual versus identified parameter and final native full-range qualification.
